Program

Authors Please Read This!

This listing is based on OpenReview data extracted at the time the acceptance notifications were sent out. If you see errors in spelling or capitalization and so on, make sure that you correct them when you are completing the ACM Rights Review Form via the link you will receive shortly.

When checking the ACM Rights Review Form you should verify the author names and affiliations very carefully. in every respect. As well, please ensure that all authors from the same institution have a consistent form of the institution's name. For example, all of "The University of Nowhere" and "University of Nowhere" and "Nowhere University" might be fine separately, but please don't have a mixture of them on the same paper.

The final version of your paper must then have exactly the same author names and affiliations, identical in every detail to the submitted Rights Review Form. Even very minor inconsistencies are likely to be queried by the ACM; and mismatches that are not rectified promptly risk delaying publication of the proceedings (maybe you don't care) or risk your paper being excluded from the proceedings (maybe you do care!).

Note that we will not be applying name/title corrections requested through the ACM Rights Review process to this preliminary acceptance listing. This page is a static snapshot of the paper titles and authors names used to initiate the ACM Rights Review process, and will not get edited further. A detailed program with corrected title and author names will be prepared once all camera-ready versions have been received by the ACM and approved, and once the papers have been assigned to sessions.

And as a reminder: camera-ready versions must be uploaded by 14 June (AoE); and registration of a presenting author is required by 14 June (Melbourne time), see https://sigir2026.org/en-AU/pages/attending/registration.

Please pay careful attention to the information about page lengths, required information, and ACM Author Page Charges that was provided in the acceptance emails.

Presentations

All papers will be presented as posters, A0 portrait format.

In addition, a selection of papers will be invited for oral presentation, with that selection made on the basis of likely interest to the anticipated audience and to span a variety of topics, and not as a measure of paper importance. All accepted papers rank equally in terms of importance, regardless of length or presentation format. The decision on the presentation format of each accepted paper will be sent by email within two weeks.
