Friday July 24, 2026

The ICTIR 2026 Conference Dinner and Awards ceremony will take place at the River's Edge Restaurant across the river from the Melbourne Convention and Exhibition Center commencing at 6:30pm pm.

All ICTIR 2026 sessions will take place in the Eureka 1/2/3 Room at the Melbourne Convention and Exhibition Center.

DOI links to the ACM Digital Library will be activated shortly prior to the day of the conference.
